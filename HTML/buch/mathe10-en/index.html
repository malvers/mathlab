CONTENTSIN THE ORDER OF THE SCHOOL YEAR

The chapters follow the teaching plan of Year 10: first triangles and polygons (learning area 1) with the sine and cosine rules, then functions (learning area 2) from the hyperbola to the sine curve, and random variables (learning area 3) with the expected value. Learning area 4 brings mathematics into everyday life: wages, saving and credit, solids and frustums, and a chapter on problem solving for the exam. The compulsory elective area takes you outside with the forester’s triangle and the measuring wheel. On the right you can see when each chapter is due and how many self-checks you have already solved.

HOW TO USE THIS BOOK
EVERY CHAPTER HAS THE SAME STRUCTURE
  1. Opener with learning goals and a check-in: what you should already be able to do.
  2. Explorations: open questions to try out, ideally in groups.
  3. Units: starter, explanation, key-point box, examples, interactive elements, exercises and “Test yourself!”.
  4. Review · Deepen · Connect: mixed exercises.
  5. Excursion: history, an application or a nice puzzle.
  6. Looking back on one page and a test with two rounds.
THE SYMBOLS

Basic   Intermediate   Advanced

Every exercise has the solution to fold out, some also a hint. Try it yourself first.

In the self-checks you type in your result. Fractions ($3/10$), decimals ($0.3$) and percentages ($30\,\%$) are all understood. Your browser remembers what you have solved.

Whatever is marked with a calculator, you work out with a calculator. Whatever is marked without aids, you practise for the part of the exam without a calculator.

Labs open in a new tab. There you can experiment for longer and more freely than in the book. The labs and the weekly exercise sheets are in German for now.

KEY POINT

Whatever is in an orange box, you need to know. Everything else helps you to understand it.
