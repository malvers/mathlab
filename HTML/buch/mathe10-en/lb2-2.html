Exponential functions

Savings with compound interest, a culture of bacteria, a medicine in the blood, the loss in value of a mobile phone: here the same amount is not added every year, but the value is multiplied by the same factor. That is exponential growth, described by $y = c \cdot a^x$.

UNIT 12 LESSONS

1Exponential growth

Offer A brings €300 after 30 days. With offer B, the 30th day alone brings $0.01 \cdot 2^{29} \approx 5.4$ million €. With A the same summand is added every day: linear growth. With B the value is multiplied by the same factor every day: exponential growth.

KEY POINT · EXPONENTIAL FUNCTION

$y = c \cdot a^x$ with the initial value $c$ (at $x = 0$) and the growth factor $a > 0$.

Increase by $p\,\%$: $a = 1 + \dfrac{p}{100}$. $\;$ Decrease by $p\,\%$: $a = 1 - \dfrac{p}{100}$.

$a > 1$: growth, $0 < a < 1$: decrease. All graphs $y = a^x$ pass through $(0, 1)$, the x-axis is an asymptote.

How to recognise it in a table: equal quotients of consecutive values. Linear: equal differences.

Power or exponential function? In $x^2$ the variable is in the base, in $2^x$ in the exponent.

Rule of thumb: with $p\,\%$ growth a value doubles after about $\tfrac{70}{p}$ steps.

INTERACTIVE · GROWTH MODELS

Compare linear and exponential growth. Change the initial value and the factor, and watch when the exponential function overtakes.

EXAMPLE 1 · COMPOUND INTEREST

€1000 is invested at 3 %, and the interest earns interest too. How much is it after 10 years?

$K(10) = 1000 \cdot 1.03^{10} \approx$ €1343.92. Without compound interest it would be €1300.

EXAMPLE 2 · EQUATION FROM TWO VALUES

A population is 50 at the start and 75 after one year. Give the equation for exponential growth.

$c = 50$, $a = \tfrac{75}{50} = 1.5$: $y = 50 \cdot 1.5^x$. After 3 years: $168.75$.

A culture of bacteria doubles every hour. At the start there are 500. How many are there after 5 and after 10 hours?

$500 \cdot 2^5 = 16{,}000$, $500 \cdot 2^{10} = 512{,}000$.

A town has 10,000 inhabitants, and the number grows by 1.5 % per year. How many are there after 4 years? After about how many years 20,000?

$10{,}000 \cdot 1.015^4 \approx 10{,}614$. Doubling after about $70 : 1.5 \approx 47$ years (exactly: 46.6).

A channel has 100 followers, and the number doubles every month. How many would there be after one year, after two years? Evaluate the model.

$409{,}600$ and about 1.7 billion – roughly every fifth person on earth. Exponential growth always reaches limits.

2Decrease and half-life

In the dice experiment about $\tfrac56$ of the dice are left after each throw. The number decreases by the factor $\tfrac56 \approx 0.83$. After four throws there are $100 \cdot \left(\tfrac56\right)^4 \approx 48$: about half. This time is called the half-life.

KEY POINT · DECAY

With exponential decrease the amount always halves in the same time, the half-life $T$.

After $n$ half-lives, $c \cdot \left(\tfrac12\right)^n$ is still left.

Decreases are not added: 20 % less twice gives $0.8 \cdot 0.8 = 0.64$, so 36 % less, not 40 %.

Loss in value: in its first years a car often loses about a fifth of its value per year – each time of the remaining value.

PICTURE · HALVING IN EQUAL STEPS

80 mg of a medicine with a half-life of 6 hours: every 6 hours the rest halves.

EXAMPLE 3 · THE MEDICINE

How much of 80 mg is still there after 18 hours, with a half-life of 6 hours?

18 hours are three half-lives: $80 \cdot 0.5^3 =$ 10 mg.

EXAMPLE 4 · LOSS IN VALUE

A smartphone for €800 loses 20 % of its value every year. What is it worth after 2 years?

$800 \cdot 0.8^2 =$ €512. Anyone who subtracts $2 \cdot 20\,\%$ wrongly gets €480.

20 % of a medicine is broken down per hour. How much of 100 mg is left after 3 hours?

Exponential $\approx 75{,}295$, linear 75,200. With exponential decrease the yearly loss becomes a little smaller.

A price is first raised by 20 % and then lowered by 20 %. How does it compare with the original price?

$1.2 \cdot 0.8 = 0.96$: 4 % lower.

In the dice experiment $\tfrac56$ are left after each throw. Explain why half are gone after about 4 throws. After how many throws is less than a tenth left?

$\left(\tfrac56\right)^4 \approx 0.48$. $\left(\tfrac56\right)^{12} \approx 0.112$, $\left(\tfrac56\right)^{13} \approx 0.093$: after 13 throws.

$5000 \cdot 1.03^5 \approx 5796.37$ €, so $796.37$ € interest.

Connecting: compare $y = x^2$ and $y = 2^x$ for $x = 1$ to $6$. From when on is $2^x$ always larger?

1|2, 4|4, 9|8, 16|16, 25|32, 36|64. From $x = 5$ on, $2^x$ is always larger.

EXCURSIONAN OLD LEGEND

The grains of rice on the chessboard

The inventor of chess is said to have asked the king for this reward: one grain on the first square, two on the second, four on the third and always twice as many up to the 64th square. The king laughed at the modest request.

On the last square there are $2^{63}$ grains, together $2^{64} - 1 \approx 1.8 \cdot 10^{19}$. That is many times the amount of grain harvested in the whole world in one year today. The legend has come down to us in many versions, one of the oldest from the scholar Ibn Khallikan in the 13th century.

Source: Ibn Khallikan: Wafayat al-ayan (biographical dictionary), around 1256; after H. J. R. Murray: A History of Chess, Oxford 1913.
