UNIT 1WITHOUT A CALCULATOR · 5 LESSONS

1Solving linear equations, rearranging formulas

A taxi costs a basic fare of €4 and €2.20 per kilometre. Lisa paid €26 for her ride. How far did she go?

For the distance $x$ (in km) we have $4 + 2.2x = 26$. A number that gives a true statement when substituted is called a solution of the equation. All solutions together form the solution set $L$.

As with a balance, you may do the same on both sides without changing the solution set. Such steps are called equivalence transformations. You write them after a vertical bar:

$$4 + 2.2x = 26 \quad \big|\; -4 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad 2.2x = 22 \quad \big|\; :2.2 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad x = 10$$

Lisa travelled 10 km. Check: $4 + 2.2 \cdot 10 = 26$ is true.

KEY POINT · EQUIVALENCE TRANSFORMATIONS

The solution set of an equation does not change if on both sides you

  • add or subtract the same number or the same term,
  • multiply or divide by the same number other than zero.

Route map: expand brackets and simplify · all $x$ on one side · all numbers on the other · divide by the number in front of $x$ · check.

Careful: multiplying by $0$ is forbidden. $x = 3$ would turn into $0 = 0$, and the information is gone.

Special cases: if $x$ disappears completely, you are left with a true statement ($L = \mathbb{R}$) or a contradiction ($L = \{\,\}$).

INTERACTIVE · TRANSFORMATION TRAINER

Type the transformation into the field, for example -3, +2x, :4 or ·2. The trainer applies it to both sides. Goal: $x$ stands alone.

A formula is an equation with several variables. You rearrange it for one quantity by treating all the other quantities like numbers and using the same equivalence transformations.

EXAMPLE 1 · WITH BRACKETS

Solve $3(x - 2) + 4 = 5x - 8$.

$3x - 6 + 4 = 5x - 8$ (expand the bracket)

$3x - 2 = 5x - 8 \quad \big|\; -5x$

$-2x - 2 = -8 \quad \big|\; +2$

$-2x = -6 \quad \big|\; :(-2)$

$x = 3$. Check: left $3 \cdot 1 + 4 = 7$, right $15 - 8 = 7$.

EXAMPLE 2 · REARRANGING A FORMULA

For free fall, $s = \tfrac{a}{2}\,t^2$. Rearrange for $t$. How long does a stone take to fall 20 m ($a = 9.81\,\tfrac{\text{m}}{\text{s}^2}$)?

$s = \tfrac{a}{2}\,t^2 \quad \big|\; \cdot \tfrac{2}{a} \qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad t^2 = \tfrac{2s}{a} \qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad t = \sqrt{\tfrac{2s}{a}}$ (time is positive)

$t = \sqrt{\tfrac{2 \cdot 20}{9.81}}\,\text{s} \approx 2.02\,\text{s}$

After 10 s of free fall it would have the speed $v = 9.81\,\tfrac{\text{m}}{\text{s}^2} \cdot 10\,\text{s} = 98.1\,\tfrac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}$.

UNIT 2WITHOUT A CALCULATOR · 5 LESSONS

2Reasoning, percentages, binomial formulas

A cube-shaped container holds 8 litres. How long is one edge? And how often do you have to fold a sheet of paper until 16 layers lie on top of each other?

Not every equation needs transformations. For $a^3 = 8$ you simply ask: which number cubed gives 8? That is $a = 2$, so the edge is 2 dm long. For $2^k = 16$ you ask: how often do you have to multiply 2 by itself? $k = 4$. This way of solving is called solving by reasoning.

Careful with even powers: $x^2 = 9$ has two solutions, $x = 3$ and $x = -3$.

KEY POINT · PERCENTAGES AND BINOMIAL FORMULAS

Percent: percentage value $W = G \cdot \tfrac{p}{100}$ (base value $G$). An increase by $p\%$ means: times $\left(1 + \tfrac{p}{100}\right)$, a decrease by $p\%$: times $\left(1 - \tfrac{p}{100}\right)$.

Directly proportional: $y = k \cdot x$, the quotient $\tfrac{y}{x}$ is constant. Inversely proportional: $y = \tfrac{k}{x}$, the product $x \cdot y$ is constant.

Binomial formulas:

$$(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \qquad (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \qquad (a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$$

Careful: 20% more and then 20% less does not give the old value: $1.2 \cdot 0.8 = 0.96$, i.e. 4% less.

Mental arithmetic: $49 \cdot 51 = (50 - 1)(50 + 1) = 2500 - 1 = 2499$.

EXAMPLE 3 · DISCOUNT AND MARK-UP

A phone costs €480. In a sale there is a 15% discount. Afterwards the price goes up again by 15%. Does it then cost €480 again?

EXAMPLE 4 · BINOMIALS BACKWARDS

Write as a product: a) $x^2 - 6x + 9$   b) $25 - 4y^2$

UNIT 3WITH DIGITAL TOOLS · 5 LESSONS

3Equations with digital tools

Solve $x^3 + x = 5$. Transforming gets you nowhere, you cannot get $x$ on its own on one side. Still, there is a solution. How do you find it?

For equations like this you use digital tools: a CAS calculator, a graphing calculator, GeoGebra or DocPad. There are three ways:

  • algebraic (exact): the CAS transforms the equation and returns fractions, roots or $\pi$, for example $x = \sqrt{2}$.
  • graphical: you draw both sides as functions and read off the point of intersection.
  • numerical (approximation): the tool closes in on the solution step by step and returns a decimal, for example $x \approx 1.516$.
KEY POINT · LEVELS OF A TOOL

Exact means: without rounding, with roots, fractions and $\pi$. Numerical means: a rounded decimal.

Work exactly for as long as possible and round only at the very end. Check every result from a tool for plausibility: is the order of magnitude right? Does the check work?

GeoGebra: Solve(x^3+x=5) tries it exactly, NSolve(x^3+x=5) numerically.

Lab: DocPad is a CAS and graphing calculator in the browser, with fractions, roots and $\pi$.

SOLVING GRAPHICALLY · $x^3 + x = 5$

The solution is the $x$-coordinate of the point where $y = x^3 + x$ and $y = 5$ intersect.

EXAMPLE 5 · EXACT OR ROUNDED?

Lena calculates $(\sqrt2)^{10}$ and rounds first: $\sqrt2 \approx 1.4$. What does she get, what would be right?

$1.4^{10} \approx 28.9$. Exactly: $(\sqrt2)^{10} = 2^5 = 32$. The small rounding error has grown by more than 10% through the powers.

EXAMPLE 6 · SAVINGS GOAL

€1,000 earns 3% interest per year. After how many years will it be €1,500? Solve $1000 \cdot 1.03^n = 1500$ numerically.

The CAS gives $n \approx 13.72$. After 14 years the goal is reached. You will learn to solve it exactly in chapter 3.6 with the logarithm.

EXERCISES

Solve exactly and numerically with a CAS: a) $x^2 = 7$   b) $3x^2 - 5 = 1$   c) $(x + 1)^3 = 20$

a) $x = \pm\sqrt7 \approx \pm 2.646$   b) $x^2 = 2$, $x = \pm\sqrt2 \approx \pm 1.414$   c) $x = \sqrt[3]{20} - 1 \approx 1.714$

Find the points of intersection of $f(x) = x^2$ and $g(x) = x + 2$ graphically. Check by calculation.

$x^2 = x + 2 \Leftrightarrow (x - 2)(x + 1) = 0$: $S_1(-1, 1)$ and $S_2(2, 4)$.

Solve $2^x = 10$ in three ways: by trial and improvement with a calculator, graphically and with a CAS. Compare effort and accuracy.

Trial: $2^3 = 8$, $2^{3.3} \approx 9.85$, $2^{3.35} \approx 10.2$ … Graphically: intersection with $y = 10$. CAS: $x = \tfrac{\ln 10}{\ln 2} \approx 3.3219$.

One CAS gives “no solution” for $x^2 + 1 = 0$, another $x = \pm i$. Find out what $i$ means and explain the difference.

Among the real numbers there is no number whose square is $-1$. Some CAS work with complex numbers, where $i$ is the number with $i^2 = -1$. So watch which number system your tool is working in.

4Calculating parts of figures and solids

Back to the can from the exploration: 330 ml, 11.5 cm tall. We want the radius, and we know the volume. So you have to rearrange a formula.

KEY POINT · THE MOST IMPORTANT FORMULAS
Figure / solidArea / volumePerimeter / surface area
Circle$A = \pi r^2$$u = 2\pi r$
Triangle$A = \tfrac12 g h$$u = a + b + c$

Pythagoras' theorem: in a right-angled triangle $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ ($c$ is the hypotenuse).

Units: $1\,\text{l} = 1\,\text{dm}^3 = 1000\,\text{cm}^3$, so $330\,\text{ml} = 330\,\text{cm}^3$.

Factoring out: $2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h = 2\pi r(r + h)$. Both forms are equivalent, the right one is quicker to calculate.

EXAMPLE 7 · THE CAN

A cylindrical can should hold 330 cm³ and be 11.5 cm tall. Calculate the radius and the amount of sheet metal needed.

$V = \pi r^2 h \;\Rightarrow\; r = \sqrt{\dfrac{V}{\pi h}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{330}{\pi \cdot 11.5}}\,\text{cm} \approx 3.02\,\text{cm}$

$S = 2\pi r(r + h) \approx 2\pi \cdot 3.02 \cdot 14.52\,\text{cm}^2 \approx 276\,\text{cm}^2$

Real cans are about 6.6 cm in diameter. They are not completely full, narrower at the top and have a domed base.

EXERCISES

A circle has radius 5 cm. Calculate its area and circumference.

$A = 25\pi \approx 78.54\,\text{cm}^2$, $u = 10\pi \approx 31.42\,\text{cm}$

A right-angled triangle has legs of 6 cm and 8 cm. Calculate the hypotenuse and the area.

$c = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10\,\text{cm}$, $A = \tfrac12 \cdot 6 \cdot 8 = 24\,\text{cm}^2$

A sphere is to hold exactly 1 litre. What is its radius?

$\tfrac43 \pi r^3 = 1000 \Rightarrow r = \sqrt[3]{\tfrac{3000}{4\pi}} \approx 6.20\,\text{cm}$

An ice-cream cone is a cone with $r = 2.5\,\text{cm}$ and $h = 12\,\text{cm}$. How much ice cream fits in (without a scoop on top)? How much wafer do you need for the curved surface?

$V = \tfrac13 \pi \cdot 2.5^2 \cdot 12 = 25\pi \approx 78.5\,\text{cm}^3$. Slant height $s = \sqrt{2.5^2 + 12^2} \approx 12.26\,\text{cm}$, curved surface $L = \pi r s \approx 96.3\,\text{cm}^2$.

A cylindrical can is to hold 1 litre. Which dimensions need the least sheet metal? Experiment with the solid calculator and a table for $r = 4, 5, 6, 8\,\text{cm}$.

From $V = \pi r^2 h = 1000$ it follows that $h = \tfrac{1000}{\pi r^2}$. Substitute this into $S = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h$: $S = 2\pi r^2 + \tfrac{2000}{r}$.

$r = 4$: $S \approx 601\,\text{cm}^2$ · $r = 5$: $S \approx 557$ · $r = 6$: $S \approx 560$ · $r = 8$: $S \approx 652$.

The least sheet metal is needed at about $r \approx 5.42\,\text{cm}$ and $h \approx 10.84\,\text{cm}$ ($S \approx 554\,\text{cm}^2$). Then the height is exactly the same as the diameter. You can prove this in Year 12 with the derivative.

Rearrange $S = 2\pi r(r + h)$ for $h$.

$\tfrac{S}{2\pi r} = r + h \Rightarrow h = \tfrac{S}{2\pi r} - r$

EXCURSIONA PUZZLE FROM ANTIQUITY

How old did Diophantus live to be?

Diophantus of Alexandria probably lived in the 3rd century and wrote the “Arithmetica”, one of the first books about equations. Almost nothing is known about his life. But a puzzle in verse has come down to us, roughly:

For a sixth of his life he was a child, for a twelfth a youth. After a further seventh he married, and five years later a son was born to him. The son reached only half the age of his father. Four years after the son, Diophantus died.

Source: Greek Anthology (Anthologia Graeca), Book XIV, Epigram 126. The collection is several hundred years younger than Diophantus.

SOLVE THE PUZZLE

Set up an equation for his age $x$ and solve it. How old did Diophantus live to be?

$x = \tfrac{x}{6} + \tfrac{x}{12} + \tfrac{x}{7} + 5 + \tfrac{x}{2} + 4$. Times 84: $84x = 14x + 7x + 12x + 420 + 42x + 336$, so $9x = 756$ and $x = 84$. Check: $14 + 7 + 12 + 5 + 42 + 4 = 84$.
