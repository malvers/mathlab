UNIT 12 LESSONS

1Laws of arithmetic and order of operations

$0.25 \cdot 7.3 \cdot 4$ looks difficult. If you swap the factors, it becomes easy: $0.25 \cdot 4 = 1$ and $1 \cdot 7.3 = 7.3$. The laws of arithmetic from Year 5 also hold for fractions and decimals.

With plus and with times you may swap and move brackets. The distributive law connects times and plus: $8 \cdot 1.25 + 8 \cdot 0.75 = 8 \cdot (1.25 + 0.75) = 8 \cdot 2 = 16$.

The order stays the same: brackets before powers before multiplication and division before addition and subtraction. Operations of the same rank are worked out from left to right.

KEY POINT · LAWS OF ARITHMETIC

Commutative law: $a + b = b + a$ and $a \cdot b = b \cdot a$

Associative law: $(a + b) + c = a + (b + c)$ and $(a \cdot b) \cdot c = a \cdot (b \cdot c)$

Distributive law: $a \cdot (b + c) = a \cdot b + a \cdot c$

Order: brackets → powers → multiplication and division → addition and subtraction

Not with minus and divided by: $5 - 2 \ne 2 - 5$ and $6 : 3 \ne 3 : 6$. There you must not swap.

Good partners: $0.25$ and 4, $0.5$ and 2, $\tfrac13$ and 3 make 1 together.

UNIT 23 LESSONS

2Mean, equations and word problems

6, 3, 8, 2 and 6 marbles make 25 marbles altogether. Shared equally, each of the five people has $25 : 5 = 5$ marbles. This number is called the arithmetic mean or the average.

You solve an equation like $3 \cdot x = 4.5$ by reasoning: which number times 3 gives 4.5? With the inverse operation $4.5 : 3 = 1.5$. A proportion like $2 : 5 = 8 : 20$ says that two ratios are equal.

For word problems a plan helps: what is given, what are you looking for? Which calculation fits? Does the result match the estimate, and is it rounded sensibly?

KEY POINT · MEAN AND EQUATIONS

Arithmetic mean = sum of all values : number of values

Equation by reasoning: $x + 2.7 = 5$ → $x = 5 - 2.7 = 2.3$   ·   $3 \cdot x = 4.5$ → $x = 4.5 : 3 = 1.5$

Proportion: $2 : 5 = \square : 20$ – from 5 to 20 is times 4, so also $2 \cdot 4 = 8$.

Check: substitute your solution. $3 \cdot 1.5 = 4.5$ ✓

The mean does not have to be a value in the list: the average of 2 and 3 is 2.5.

EXAMPLE 3 · MEAN

In one week, 18.5 °C, 21 °C, 19.5 °C, 22 °C and 24 °C were measured at midday. How warm was it on average?

Sum: 105 °C. Five values: $105 : 5 = 21$.

21 °C on average.

EXAMPLE 4 · WORD PROBLEM

A parcel weighs 2.4 kg. It contains 6 books of equal weight, and the packaging weighs 0.3 kg. How heavy is one book?

The books together: $2.4 - 0.3 = 2.1$ kg. One book: $2.1 : 6 = 0.35$ kg.

One book weighs 350 g.

The mean of five numbers is 8. Four of them are 6, 9, 7 and 10. What is the fifth?

How big must the sum of all five be?

Sum $5 \cdot 8 = 40$, known $6 + 9 + 7 + 10 = 32$, the fifth number is 8.

Which natural numbers satisfy $x \cdot 0.5 < 3$? Give the largest.

$x \cdot 0.5$ is half of $x$, and it must be less than 3: $x < 6$. So 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 – the largest is 5.

Max has €50. First he spends a fifth of it, then another €12.50. Write the calculation in one line and work out how much he has left. Make up a similar problem yourself.

$50 - \tfrac15 \cdot 50 - 12.50 = 50 - 10 - 12.50 = 27.50$ €.

REVIEW · DEEPEN · CONNECT

For 4 people you need 300 g of pasta. How much do you need for 6 people?

1 person 75 g, 6 people 450 g.

Which question does $3 \cdot 1.20 + 2 \cdot 0.80$ fit? Write a shopping story and work it out.

For example: what do 3 bread rolls at €1.20 each and 2 pretzels at €0.80 each cost? $3.60 + 1.60 = 5.20$ €.

A newspaper writes: “A third of the 600 people at our school come by bus, a quarter by bike, the rest on foot.” How many come on foot?

Bus 200, bike 150, on foot $600 - 350 = 250$. As a fraction: $1 - \tfrac13 - \tfrac14 = \tfrac{5}{12}$, and $\tfrac{5}{12} \cdot 600 = 250$.

EXCURSIONAPPLICATION

The average in the weather

When the weather report says that October was “too warm”, the German Weather Service (Deutscher Wetterdienst) compares the measured monthly mean temperature with a long-term mean. For this you take the means of many years, for example from 1991 to 2020, and take the average of those once more.

The daily mean is an average too: today the temperature is usually measured every hour, and the sum of the 24 values is divided by 24.

Try it yourself: On one day, 4 °C, 6 °C, 13 °C and 9 °C were measured at 0:00, 6:00, 12:00 and 18:00. Calculate the mean of these four measurements.

$4 + 6 + 13 + 9 = 32$, $32 : 4 = 8$. The mean is 8 °C.

Source: Deutscher Wetterdienst, climate reference period 1991–2020 (the reference period of the World Meteorological Organization).
