How to use this book

This book exists twice: as an online book in which you can try out every formula, stretch every triangle and run every random experiment a hundred times, and as the print edition in your hands. Every chapter begins with a QR code that takes you straight to its interactive version. The figures in this book show the interactive elements in their starting state.

Every chapter has the same structure: an opener with learning goals and a check-in, explorations to try out in groups, units with a starter, key-point box, examples, exercises and “Test yourself!”, plus Review · Deepen · Connect, an excursion, a look back and a test.

Exercises come in three levels: basic, intermediate, advanced. The solutions to all exercises and self-checks are in the appendix; the worked solutions of the examples are right in the text.

Whatever is in an orange key-point box, you need to know. Everything else helps you to understand it.
