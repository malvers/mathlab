A maths book that calculates with you

Make the rectangles under a curve narrower and narrower until the sum turns into an integral, and watch the water level in a tank grow from a rate of inflow. Toss a coin a thousand times to check whether it is biased, and turn a plane in real 3D until you see the angle at which a line pierces it. On top of that: exercises at three levels, self-checks with instant feedback and a whole chapter of training for the Abitur.

CONTENTSIN THE ORDER OF THE SCHOOL YEAR

The chapters follow the teaching plan of Year 12: first integral calculus (learning area 5) until the autumn holidays, then the elective area numerical integration and inferential statistics (learning area 6) until the first exam. In the second half of the year come distances and angles in space (learning area 7) and the connected applications with extreme values, reflections and families (learning area 8). In April you train for the Abitur. On the right you can see when each chapter is due and how many self-checks you have already solved.

HOW TO USE THIS BOOK
EVERY CHAPTER HAS THE SAME STRUCTURE
  1. Opener with learning goals and a check-in: what you should already be able to do.
  2. Explorations: open questions to try out, ideally in groups.
  3. Units: starter, explanation, key-point box, examples, interactive elements, exercises and “Test yourself!”.
  4. Review · Deepen · Connect: mixed exercises.
  5. Excursion: history, an application or a nice puzzle.
  6. Looking back on one page and a test with two rounds.
THE SYMBOLS

Every exercise has a hint and the solution to fold out. Try it yourself first.

In the self-checks you type in your result. Fractions ($3/10$), decimals ($0.3$) and percentages ($30\%$) are all understood. Your browser remembers what you have solved.

Labs open in a new tab. There you can experiment for longer and more freely than in the book. The labs and the weekly exercise sheets are in German for now.

KEY POINT

Whatever is in an orange box, you need to know. Everything else helps you to understand it.
