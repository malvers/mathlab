How the Vergleichsarbeit is built

The Vergleichsarbeit (state-wide comparison test) is written by all students in Year 11 at the Berufliches Gymnasium, on the same day throughout the state with the same tasks, in 2027 on Friday, 19 March. It is marked like a class test. This chapter shows what the analysis of the last four years revealed.

90 MINUTES45 BE2021/22 TO 2024/25

The bar at the top shows the marks (Bewertungseinheiten, BE) of the 2024/25 paper, 45 marks in total. The structure and the aids are the same as for the BLF at the Gymnasium in part 2 of this book.

STRUCTUREAS IN THE LAST FOUR YEARS

1Part A and part B

PART A · WITHOUT AIDS

Only drawing instruments and the dictionary are allowed. You work directly on the task sheet; part A is collected after 25 minutes.

  • 6 marks: six multiple-choice questions with five options, 1 mark each
  • 9 marks: two short tasks; the working must be easy to follow
PART B · WITH MMS AND FORMULA BOOKLET

For 30 marks there are about 65 minutes. All three tasks must be done; there is no choice. Up to 2022/23 a graphing calculator was allowed instead of the MMS.

  • Task 1: a function
  • Task 2: a solid and a probability, in 2022/23 trigonometry instead
  • Task 3: a long real-life task set in Saxony, across all areas
KEY POINT · PRESENTATION AND APPROXIMATE VALUES

For presentation at most 2 marks are deducted, and only for serious or repeated lapses.

Where an exact value is expected, a correct approximate value still earns full marks.
