# Glossario — the Italian editions (BGY 11, 12, 13; round 2: FOS 11, FOS 12, GY 11, GY 11 LK, GY 12, GY 12 LK)

Package IT0, 18.10.2026. Every term of `tools/buch-es/glosario.md` that the three BGY books need, decided once in Italian
for `mathe11-it`, `mathe12-it` and `mathe13-it` (sections 1–5 and the Spanish blocks es-1, es-2, es-3, folded in below).
Standard Italian, the pupil is **tu**, real accents (à è é ì ò ù), the elision apostrophe is ’.
A book that needs a missing term adds it in its own block at the end (`## mathe11-it` …); an existing line is never
changed without saying so in the report.

Columns: German · Italian · note. In headings (`b-band-tag`, `b-box-title`, kickers) the Italian goes in capitals with the
accents kept (`CAPITOLO`, `LEZIONE`, `PERCHÉ`), as Orbitron draws them.

## 4. Numbers, notation, tools

- Decimal comma as in German: `0,5`; thousands with a point (`10.000`) or a thin space in TeX (`10\,000`). `data-ans`
  exactly as in the German book.
- Percent with a space as in the German book: `30 %`.
- Points and intervals as in Spanish: `P(2, 3)`, `[a, b]`; as soon as a coordinate has a decimal comma, a semicolon:
  `P(1,5; 2)`, `[0,5; 3]`; in TeX `P(1{,}5;\, 2)`. `js/buch-it.js` does it for what widgets write; the page text is
  written that way by hand.
- Months in lower case in running text (`settembre`, `ottobre · novembre`, `fine settembre`); in `kapitel.js` with a
  capital (shown in capitals anyway).
- `sin x` stays `sin x` (also in running text, as KaTeX writes `\sin`); `sen` is not used.
- GTR = calcolatrice grafica; CAS stays CAS. GeoGebra commands in their English names (Solve, NSolve, Root, If, FitLine,
  FitExp, FitPoly, Sequence, RandomBetween, CountIf), which every language version of GeoGebra accepts - said once per
  chapter where they appear. Excel in Italian: `=SE`, `=CASUALE.TRA`, `=CONTA.SE` (with `;`).
- Letters in formulas stay as in the German book (H, T, W for Hoch-, Tief-, Wendepunkt; S for the vertex), except
  where the Spanish edition changed them for the reader: identity matrix $I$, solution set $S$, outcome letters (below).
- Lab and quiz names are translated on the card, "(in tedesco)" once: Potenzlabor → LABORATORIO DELLE POTENZE.
